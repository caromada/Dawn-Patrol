HOW TO SURF
  • ← → paddle around the lineup; hold one at takeoff to pick your direction (Blacks is a left)
  • SPACE drop in when the marker says PADDLE!
  • ↓ race down the face for speed, ↑ climb back to the lip: carve rail to rail to pump
  • X snap off the lip (needs speed, high on the face). Right AT the lip with big speed it becomes an AIR
  • C cutback when you are out on the shoulder
  • F floater over a breaking section, from up near the lip
  • SPACE kick out clean to end the ride
  • Stay ahead of the curl. Stall mid-face in the pocket for the barrel.
